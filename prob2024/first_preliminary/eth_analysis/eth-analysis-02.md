## Task

Was a smart contract created to facilitate stealing? If so, obtain the `targetAddress` and `owner` defined in the contract during stealing.

## Required Information

- `<contract>`: First 10 characters of the address in lowercase format
- `<targetAddress>`: First 10 characters of the address in lowercase format
- `<owner>`: First 10 characters of the address in lowercase format

## Flag Format

`scanctf2024{smart contract:targetAddress:owner}`
